OT Checklist Guide

Simple step-by-step Checklist procedure

1. Check Overtime (OT) Hours & Numbers

Verify decimal points, odd numbers, and shifts greater than 8 hours against source documents.

Overtime Verification Procedure:

Cross-check all calculations directly against the main individual time sheet PDF:

  • Check Decimal OT Values: Verify all decimal overtime figures (e.g., 4.5, 5.5, 7.5) precisely from the individual time sheet PDF.
  • Verify Odd OT Values: Inspect all odd overtime numbers (e.g., 1, 3, 5, 7, 9) for proper supervisor authorization.
  • Verify Over 8 Hours OT: Checklist any shift exceeding 8 hours of overtime against the individual time sheet PDF to guarantee accuracy and policy compliance.

2. Fix Validation Errors

Review and resolve any flagged errors before final approval.

Validation Error Resolution Procedure:

Follow these steps to evaluate flagged exceptions:

  • Review Error Sheet: Open the automated validation error tab and inspect records flagged for irregular hour variances or improper approvals.
  • Compare with PDF: Cross-reference flagged entries with original source PDFs to determine if manual administrative intervention is required.

3. Check Manual CPL Approvals from Backend

Verify compensatory leave and overtime adjustments noted in manager remarks.

CPL Approval Verification Procedure:

Follow these steps to inspect backend data and manager notes:

  • Check Zero-OT Remarks: Review entries where overtime is approved as zero (0) but manager remarks specify compensatory leave (e.g., "2 hours CPL", "8 hours CPL").
  • Verify Mixed Approvals: Inspect combined notes (e.g., "8 hours CPL + 8 hours OT") to ensure proper credit splitting and tracking.
  • Backend System Checklist: Cross-verify these manual remarks against backend system records to ensure accurate payroll calculation and compliance.

4. Separate Executive Staff

Details and procedure for handling and filtering executive records.

Executive Staff Separation Procedure:

Follow these guidelines to manage executive records properly:

  • Copy all employees listed under the executive roster.
  • Paste them into the designated OVERTIME_SHEET tracking file.
  • Filter records thoroughly and check for any duplicate profile entries.
  • Ensure all executive staff members are completely removed from the primary non-executive calculation sheets.

5. Add Employee Details

Add extra employee information to the final report.

Employee Details Integration Procedure:

Follow these steps to append supplementary data:

  • Match Employee Records: Compare employee IDs and names against the company's official master database.
  • Add Extra Info: Append comprehensive details such as job title (designation), employee grade, and department mapping next to each entry.

6. Format All Sheets & Check Formulas

Format all sheets as per requirements and verify all formulas work properly from A to Z.

Formatting & Formula Check Procedure:

Follow these steps to ensure clean formatting and error-free calculations:

  • Format All Sheets: Ensure every sheet is formatted properly according to company requirements (proper alignments, row/column styling, and neat layout).
  • Check Formulas (A to Z): Verify all Excel formulas across columns A to Z are functioning correctly, with dynamic ranges and no broken references or static cell locks.
